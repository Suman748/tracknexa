What is TrackNexa?

TrackNexa is a vehicle tracking platform. It shows where each of your vehicles is, how fast it’s going and when it will arrive, all on one live map of Tamil Nadu.

The name joins “track” with “nexus”: the one place where your vehicles, drivers and customers connect.

Fleet ownersSee every vehicle and trip in one place.
DispatchersSpot delays early and reassign work.
CustomersGet a clear arrival time for their delivery.

How it works

  1. Install a tracker

    Fit a GPS device in the vehicle or use the driver app.

  2. Vehicle sends its location

    Position and speed are sent every few seconds.

  3. See it on the map

    Vehicles move live on the map and the dashboard updates.

  4. Get alerts and arrival times

    Delays are flagged and customers see an estimated arrival.
